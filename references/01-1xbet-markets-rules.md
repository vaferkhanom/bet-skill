# 01 — 1xBet Football Markets, Settlement & Bet-Slip Types

> 18+ only. Education only. Verify current 1xBet terms in your locale — rules vary by mirror/country and change over time. Always check the `i` tooltip on the exact market before staking.

Top matches carry **500–1,461+ markets**, grouped as Popular / 1X2 / Total / Handicap / Goals / Players / 1st-2nd Half / Corners / Cards / Specials. Live offers 30+ per match.

## 1. Settlement basis — the single most important rule

**Default = 90 minutes + referee stoppage time ONLY. No extra-time, no penalties.**

| Market group | Settles on |
|---|---|
| 1X2, Double Chance, DNB / AH 0.0, Handicap, Totals, Team Totals, BTTS, Exact Score, HT/FT, Corners, Cards, Offsides, Fouls, Shots, Goalscorer, Goal timing, Next Goal | 90 min + stoppage |
| Extra-Time markets (ET W1/X/W2, ET totals) | ET period only, score reset 0-0 |
| Penalty Shootout markets | Shootout only |
| Team To Qualify / To Go Next Round / Cup Winner | 90 min + ET + penalties |

If you want ET/pens, you must pick the `To Qualify`, `ET – …`, `Shootout` market explicitly.

## 2. Result markets

### 2.1 1X2 — Match Result
Home (1) / Draw (X) / Away (2) in regular time. Example: W1=1.631, X=4.18, W2=6.14. €10 on W1 returns €16.31. Draw is live — harder than 2-way markets.

### 2.2 1X2 2UP — early-payout variant
Separate market entry. If backed team goes **2 goals ahead at any point in regular time**, bet settles as winner immediately even if it later draws/loses. Example: same fixture standard W1 1.631 vs 2UP W1 1.556 — lower price = cost of protection. Draw cannot trigger. VAR-cancelled goals do not count. You must select the 2UP market — standard 1X2 has NO auto payout.

### 2.3 Double Chance — 1X, 12, X2
Cover 2 of 3 outcomes. 1X = home or draw, 12 = home or away (no draw), X2 = draw or away. Example: 1X=1.159, 12=1.271, 2X=2.424. 90 min only. Lower odds than 1X2. Note: `AH +0.5` ≈ Double Chance but often 0.02 better — always compare.

### 2.4 Draw No Bet (DNB) = AH 0.0 = Handicap 0
Pick winner, stake refunded at 1.00 if draw. Always shorter than 1X2 win. Check both DNB tab and AH 0.0 — take better price.

### 2.5 Team To Qualify / To Go To Next Round
Who progresses including ET + pens. Example: Qualify 1.30/3.45 vs 90-min W1 1.631 — different questions, different prices. Do not confuse.

## 3. Handicaps

### 3.1 European Handicap (3-way, whole numbers)
Virtual head-start with 3 outcomes, exact margin required, NO refund. Example EH (2-0) on favourite: must win by exactly the stated margin band — 3-0/1-0 patterns lose depending on direction. Marked `Handicap` / `European Handicap`.

### 3.2 2-way Handicap (fractional, e.g. -1.5 / +1.5)
Half-line, no draw. `F1(-1.5)` needs win by 2+. Win/draw/loss by 1 = lose.

### 3.3 Asian Handicap (whole / half / quarter)
2-way with push / half-win / half-loss. Quarter lines split stake:
- Whole (-1.0, +1.0): push (refund) if adjusted score level. Back -1.0, win 1-0 = refund.
- Half (-0.5, +1.5): no refund.
- Quarter: -0.25 = 0.0 + -0.5; -0.75 = -0.5 + -1.0; -1.25 = -1.0 + -1.5. Example $400 on +1.75 = $200 at +1.5 + $200 at +2.0. Lose by 1 = both win. Lose by 2 = +1.5 loses, +2.0 refunds. Lose by 3+ = both lose.
Push legs in accumulators settle at 1.00. 1xBet shows quarter as single number e.g. `2(-0.75)`.

Quick map: `F1(0)` = DNB. `F1(+1)` = win/draw win, lose by 1 = refund, lose by 2+ = lose. `F1(-1.5)` = need 2+.

## 4. Totals

### 4.1 Total Over/Under (match goals)
Over/Under 0.5/1.5/2.5/3.5. Over 2.5 = 3+ goals. Example Total 2.5 Over 1.768 / Under 2.124. 90 min only. Top fixtures offer 100+ total lines including Asian Total with quarter lines and half-refund (2.25, 2.75).

### 4.2 Team Total / Individual Total
Goals by one team only. Also 1st/2nd half team totals. 90 min.

### 4.3 Exact Total / Bands / Odd-Even / Goal Minutes
Exact 0/1/2/3/4+, bands 2-3, Odd/Even. Own goals count. Goal-minutes = sum of minute numbers (1H added = 45, 2H added = 90).

## 5. BTTS, Exact Score, HT/FT, Halves

- **BTTS Yes/No:** both score 1+ or not. Example Yes 1.807 / No 2.026. 90 min. Often combined BTTS + Over 2.5.
- **Exact Score:** exact 90-min score e.g. 2-1. Highest margin, highest variance.
- **HT/FT (9 combos 1/1,1/X,1/2,X/1,X/X,X/2,2/1,2/X,2/2):** first = HT leader (45 min), second = FT (90 min). Both must hit. Example 0-0 HT + 2-0 FT = X/1, not 1/1.
- **Half markets:** HT Result/Total/Handicap, 2nd Half (score reset 0-0), Highest Scoring Half, Win Either/Both Halves. ET excluded.

## 6. Goal timing / method

- **Time of First Goal:** 10/15-min bands + No Goal. If abandoned after first goal stands; before first goal, completed losing bands lose, band of abandonment voids.
- **Goal Interval / To Score in Interval / First Team Goal in Interval.**
- **Next Goal (1/X/No goal), How Goal Scored:** Free-kick direct (deflection only if taker credited), Penalty (must score directly, rebound = no), Own Goal, Header (last touch head), Shot (other), No Goal. `Team to score penalty Yes` loses if no penalty awarded. `How goal N scored` loses if goal N never comes.
- **First/Last Team to Score, Both Halves Over 0.5, To Score in Both Halves.** 90 min.

## 7. Player props

Anytime / First / Last Scorer, Player Total >0.5, Double/Hat-trick, Keeper to Score, Player Card.
- Own goals EXCLUDED from scorer markets. If opener is own goal, First Scorer = first non-own-goal scorer.
- Player must participate — DNP = void (1.00), including if match interrupted before he appears.
- Cards after FT, to benched/subbed-off players or managers = not counted.
- Penalty rebound after miss does NOT count as penalty method.

## 8. Corners / Cards / Stats

- **Corners (Total O/U, Team, First/Last/Next, Handicap, Race to N):** regular time only. **Awarded but not taken = NOT counted.** Per official protocol.
- **Cards/Bookings/Points (Total, Team, Player, First Card):** Yellow = 1 card / 10 pts. Direct Red or 2nd Yellow→Red = 2 cards / 25 pts. 2nd Yellow itself not counted separately. One player max 3 cards / 35 pts. Only on-pitch players/coaches in regular time. Post-match void.
- **Offsides, Fouls, Shots on Target/Goal, Misses, Crossbar, Blocked Shots, Penalties Awarded/Scored/Missed, Sendings-off:** per official report incl. VAR, regular time only.

## 9. Combination / Specials / Outrights

- **Combination (same-game combo):** e.g. `W1 and BTTS Yes` 3.0 vs standalone W1 1.631 — stricter, higher price. Also 1X2+Total. Compare vs multiplying singles; related outcomes cannot be acca'd manually (lowest-odds leg excluded) — use Constructor/Bet Builder.
- **Outrights:** League Winner, Top-4, Relegation, Top Scorer, Group Qualify. Settle within ~10 days of official publication; no-show/forfeit = 1.00.

## 10. Bet-slip types

| Type | Rule |
|---|---|
| Single | stake × odds. Lowest margin product. |
| Accumulator (Express) | 2+ unrelated events, odds multiply. One leg fails = all loses. Void = 1.00, acca continues. Cannot contain correlated same-match legs. Max return €50,000/bet. |
| System (2/3, 3/4…) | all same-size accas from N picks. Max 20 picks / 184,756 combos. Allows misses. Payout = sum of winning accas. |
| Chain | ordered singles linked, stake rolls over via chain account. Order = slip order. Balance hits 0 = lost. |
| Advancebet | credit against potential returns of unsettled bets. Live or pre-match starting ≤48h only. Covering bets must settle ≤48h after placement. Deposits after use do NOT cover. If cover insufficient → advance portion void. |
| Multibet (with/without Lobby) | set of accas + singles. Lobby (banker) must win. ≥3 selections besides Lobby. |
| Conditional | ordered blocks (singles/accas), each next stake ≤ return from previous. First loses = lose. |
| Anti-Accumulator | wins if acca LOSES (≥1 leg loses). 2+ unrelated. Odds = inverse win prob. Void leg raises anti odds. All void = refund. |
| Lucky / Patent | Lucky = singles + all accas (2-8 picks, need ≥1 correct). Patent = all accas only, no singles (need ≥1 winning acca, i.e. ≥2 correct). |
| Promo Code | free-bet code. Winnings credited, not refundable, excluded from other promos. One per person/account/IP. |
| Constructor (single-match) | combine several markets from SAME match (AND). Void condition recalculates total down. Single only. Light-green crane icon. |
| Constructor (Line) | fantasy match: add teams/players to Home vs Away, totals summed to virtual score. Pre-match only. |
| Bet Builder (app) | same-match combo UI, combined odds in slip, cash-out often available. |

Min stake ≈ $0.30/€0.20 per selection. Max stake varies per selection; acca/system uses lowest limit. Related outcomes in acca/system → lowest-odds legs excluded. Bets accepted after server registration + confirmation — cannot alter/revoke except Sell/Edit where offered. Bets after start void unless Live. Bets on known outcomes void. Erroneous settlements recalculated.

## 11. Postponed / interrupted / abandoned

- Pre-match postponed/rescheduled **>48h** from official start = void (1.00). Book may keep standing to avoid disputes.
- Live interrupted and resumes **within 5h** from start = stands. Else = 1.00 except already-decided outcomes.
- Abandoned: decided outcomes stand (1st Half, First Goal + time, Over 0.5 after a goal); undetermined = 1.00.
- Participant withdraws before start = void that participant. Drops out during contest = bets before last round he played stand.
- Settlement per official result/governing body. Early settlement may be recalculated.

## 12. Live rules

Markets suspend after goals, red/yellow-red, penalties; short acceptance delay; totals/handicaps repriced. If market stays open after event already happened — 1xBet may void. Live singles/accas allowed on main + additional. Cash-Out price moves while dialog open — must re-accept.

## 13. Margin / odds / Probability widget

Default decimal (switchable fractional/American/Hong Kong/Indonesian/Malay). Margin = `sum(1/odds) - 1`. Example Real vs Atletico 1.835/3.76/4.10 → 5.48% (below 6-8% avg). Typical: 1X2 ~2.3-5%, O/U 2.5 ~3.6-5.6%, BTTS ~5.5-7.5%, AH ~2-6.5%. Top leagues tighter.

**Probability of Winning widget = margin-included implied prob, rescaled to 100%.** Example 1.631/4.18/6.14 → 61.3/23.9/16.3 sum 101.5% → rescaled 60.4/23.6/16.0 = widget 60/24/16. Use as starting comparison, never as true prob. For value, remove margin first: `no_vig = implied / sum(implied)`.

## 14. Promos (check local terms — crypto/bonus-fund exclusions common)

- **Accumulator of the Day +10%:** curated accas, take as-is, main-account real money only. Win → odds ×1.10.
- **Cash Out / Bet Slip Sale (+Auto/Partial):** Singles/Accas/Systems only. Full or partial. Price = case-by-case minus 2-12% margin. Blocked if settled/blocked/unsellable/already sold/price moved. Manual sale deactivates Auto. Cannot sell insured bets.
- **Bet Insurance (paid, 10-100% of Single/Acca):** fee depends on odds/event/stake%. Win → full return (fee sunk). Lose → insured % refunded. Void at 1.00 → fee refunded. Multiple insurances up to 100% total.
- **No Risk Bet (Exact Score):** first single on promo exact-score market; lose → refund as promo-code free bet up to limit.
- **20 Losing Bets Bonus / Birthday Free Bet / Promo Store / TOTO jackpots (TOTO-15, Football-TOTO, Correct-Score, Free daily 12):** check bonus/rules pages for wagering (e.g. 5× in 3+ accas @1.40+ within 30 days — often -EV).
